This is an independent reproduction, not your cluster. The dataset is synthetic, but the constraint is modelled faithfully: your ~30 GB on an M20's 4 GB of RAM is roughly a 7:1 data-to-cache ratio, reproduced here as ~1.3 GB against a 0.25 GB WiredTiger cache. Every query below is capped — a timeout is the result.
